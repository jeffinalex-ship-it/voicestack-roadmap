Executive Summary

Strategic Pillars of 2026

Five focused investment areas designed to accelerate growth, deepen AI capabilities, and expand market reach.

☎ Phone System & Call Center

  • Outbound Dialer V1 with Preview, Power & Voice Broadcast
  • Call Conferencing, Whisper, Barge & Call Flip
  • Simultaneous Multi-Device Ringing
  • Location-Level Call Redirection Modes
  • Emergency Voicemail Delivery System
  • VIP Priority Routing & Sticky Agent
  • Wallboard 2.0 with Historical Analytics

🤖 AI Agents & Call Intelligence

  • Inbound Voice Bots for Dental, PT, Vet
  • OneAPI integrations: Open Dental, Eaglesoft, Denticon, Dentally
  • Reimagined Call Scoring with Custom Rubrics
  • PO Improvements & Conversion Probability
  • Agent Voice Detection via Vocal Biometrics
  • Bot Builder Platform & Bot Analytics

📈 CRM & Customer Engagement

  • CRM Contact Book Phase 1 with PMS Sync
  • CRM Tags, Linked Contacts & Contact Merging
  • P360 Revamp & Activity Logging
  • Email, WhatsApp & Facebook as Channels
  • Website Forms & Embeddable Chatbot
  • Advanced Automation Workflow Editor

🔗 Integrations & OneAPI

  • Bitwerx (Vet) — opens up ezyVet, Covetrus & more
  • EmpowerEMR (PT) & Prompt EMR Bot Integration
  • Dentrix Ascend / Core Neo Inbound Bot
  • Patient Records Middleware & Caching Layer
  • Vision EHR Integrations #2 & #3
  • Practice Dilly & CareStack Short Code Sync

📊 Analytics & Platform

  • Appointment Linking & Revenue Analytics
  • DNI Dashboard & Configuration Improvements
  • Bot Analytics & Bot Call Flow Logs
  • Scheduled Reports & Admin Digest
  • Date Range Limits / Data Archival
  • Audit Logs & RBAC for Enterprise

📱 Mobile & Agent Experience

  • Contact Book on Mobile & Tasks on Mobile
  • Search in Mobile App & DND on Mobile
  • Internal Chat between Agents
  • Headset Answering & Call Flip
  • Saved Table Columns & Sorting Preferences
  • Jump Pages in Agent Application
